# Rubric: grounded answer

Score 1.0 when every factual claim in the answer is supported by the retrieved
passages and the answer cites them; 0.5 when the main claim is supported but a detail
is not; 0.0 when the main claim is unsupported or contradicts the passages. Refusals
for out-of-scope questions are graded by the refusal check, not by this rubric.
